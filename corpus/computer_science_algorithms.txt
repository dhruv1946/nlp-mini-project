In mathematics and computer science, an algorithm is a finite sequence of rigorous instructions, typically used to solve a class of specific problems or to perform a computation. Algorithms are used as specifications for performing calculations and data processing. By using artificial intelligence, machine learning, and automated reasoning, algorithms can perform complex mathematical operations, automated decision-making, and pattern recognition tasks. Key algorithmic paradigms include divide and conquer, dynamic programming, greedy algorithms, and graph traversal. Efficiency is characterized by asymptotic computational complexity using Big O notation for time and space resources.
