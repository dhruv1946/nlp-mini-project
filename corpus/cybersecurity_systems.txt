Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, devices, and programs from digital attacks, unauthorized access, theft, and damage. Cyberattacks are typically aimed at accessing, altering, or destroying sensitive information, extorting money from users via ransomware, or interrupting normal business processes. Modern cybersecurity utilizes cryptographic protocols, multi-factor authentication, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and vulnerability management. As digital ecosystems grow, automated machine learning models are increasingly deployed to detect anomalous behavior and malicious network traffic.
