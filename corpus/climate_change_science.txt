Climate change describes global warming and its consequences on Earth's climate system. The current rise in global temperatures is more rapid than previous changes, and is primarily caused by humans burning fossil fuels. Fossil fuel use creates greenhouse gas emissions, mostly carbon dioxide and methane. These emissions trap heat from the Sun, causing the global surface temperature to rise. Other human activities including agriculture, deforestation, and industrial manufacturing contribute to global warming as well. The impacts of climate change include stronger heat waves, rising sea levels, changing precipitation patterns, and biodiversity loss.
